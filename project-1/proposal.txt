This project shall simulate the processing of compute jobs by
a 4-node compute cluster.

100 jobs shall be loaded from a file, represented in the following
format:

42
123
7
...

Where column 0 is the Job ID.

The compute cluster shall be represented as a map containing
arrays of three lists.

The top-level map holds compute nodes by their respective
unique IDs.

The array of lists represents three job states:
 - Pending,
 - Active, and
 - Completed

When jobs are first loaded from the file, they are queued
into the compute nodes using a round-robin approach.

On each time tick, each compute node will finish one compute job,
provided that there are jobs remaining in their respective Active queue.

When a job completes, it will be moved from Active to Completed.

If there are less than 5 jobs in the Active queue, an
appropriate amount of jobs will be transfered from Pending
into Active.

This simulation shall run for 25 time periods, as that is the amount
necessary for 100 jobs to be processed by 4 compute nodes.
